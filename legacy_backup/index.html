Our Story

WHY
FLEX?

Flex was born from a simple observation: while sunglasses were easy to find in Sri Lanka, a truly premium buying experience was not.

Founded by an undergraduate student specializing in Optometry and Marketing Management, Flex began as a passion project — bringing together quality products, luxury presentation, and exceptional customer care at reasonable prices.

Every product is carefully selected, quality-checked, and presented with attention to detail because we believe the experience should feel just as premium as the product itself.
