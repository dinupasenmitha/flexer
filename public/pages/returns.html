Customer Protection

RETURNS & EXCHANGES

We want you to feel confident and comfortable in every frame you choose. If your piece does not fit your facial dimensions as expected, we provide a structured exchange service.

1. Exchange Window

Exchanges may be requested within 48 hours of courier delivery (or up to 14 days for unopened, sealed gift sets).

2. Condition Requirements

  • Eyewear must be completely unworn, with zero scratches or temple distortions.
  • Must include all original packaging: hard case / zip pouch, certificate, and microfibre cloth.

3. Exchange Process

To initiate an exchange, reach out directly on our official WhatsApp Care (+94 77 558 0968) with your order reference (e.g., FLX-2026-XXXXXX) and photos of the frame. Our team will guide you on the nearest courier drop or swap.
