SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

At FLEXER, all eyewear pieces are inspected for optical alignment and securely packed before dispatch from our Colombo fulfilment hub.

1. Delivery Coverage

We deliver to all 25 administrative districts across Sri Lanka via trusted domestic courier partners.

2. Delivery Timeframes

  • Colombo & Greater Colombo: 1 to 2 business days.
  • Outstation Districts (Kandy, Galle, Kurunegala, Jaffna, etc.): 2 to 4 business days.

3. Delivery Charges

Currently, FLEXER is offering Free Islandwide Delivery on all standard eyewear orders. Any future promotional changes or express delivery options will be clearly displayed during checkout prior to order submission.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, our support team will provide your courier tracking number via WhatsApp / SMS. You may also track directly by providing your FLX-2026-XXXXXX order reference to our WhatsApp Care line.
