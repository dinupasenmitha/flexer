What is Covered

  • Hinge weld separation or defective factory screws.
  • Spontaneous lens polarization peeling or delamination under normal wear.
  • Acetate core-wire detachment.

What is Excluded

Normal cosmetic wear and tear, accidental dropping, crushing, scratches resulting from cleaning with abrasive materials or improper chemical exposure.

How to Claim

Simply WhatsApp your Order Reference (e.g. FLX-2026-XXXXXX) and photos of the frame to (+94 77 558 0968). Our team will evaluate and arrange repair or replacement.
