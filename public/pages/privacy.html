Trust & Security

CUSTOMER PRIVACY NOTICE

FLEXER Eyewear takes customer trust and privacy seriously. This document outlines how we collect, handle, and protect your information when ordering through our online boutique.

Strict Payment Slip Privacy Guarantee

When you select a bank payment receipt on our checkout confirmation, the FLEXER website operates a browser-local file interface.

  • Payment slip files are NEVER uploaded to our web servers.
  • Slips are NEVER stored in our databases or cloud storage.
  • Slips are NEVER embedded into persistent order logs or server caches.
  • Files are shared strictly by you through your native device share or directly inside WhatsApp.

1. What Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect only the essential details necessary to fulfil and deliver your package:

  • Full customer name.
  • Delivery address, city, and Sri Lankan administrative district.
  • Telephone number and WhatsApp contact number for courier delivery coordination.
  • Purchased items snapshot and chosen payment method.

2. How Information is Used

Your information is solely used to verify order payments, arrange dispatch with registered domestic couriers, and provide customer support via WhatsApp. We never sell, rent, or share customer data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security

All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS (SSL/TLS). Privileged admin access requires authenticated HMAC token verification.
