# FLEXER content and launch checklist

Everything below is intentionally a placeholder or needs business approval before launch.

## Required business configuration

- Bank account holder, bank name, branch, account number, and transfer instructions.
- Official WhatsApp community invitation URL. The configuration is intentionally a placeholder until FLEXER supplies the approved invite.
- Koko merchant ID, API key, webhook secret, and explicit approval to enable Koko. Keep `KOKO_ENABLED=0` until all are received and validated.
- Delivery-fee rules, shipping cutoff times, and any promotional banner copy.
- Registered business identity and support email for legal and transactional pages.

## Catalogue and imagery

- Replace `database/seed/products.json` with approved product names, SKUs, prices, variants, stock, dimensions, materials, lens specifications, inclusions, warranty durations, and descriptions.
- Replace placeholder catalogue images with owned FLEXER product photography. Supply at least a primary and hover image for each saleable variant.
- Confirm that every UV400, polarization, Italian-acetate, material, and warranty statement is backed by the actual product specification.
- Supply original customer testimonials with permission to publish. Do not publish invented reviews.

## Hero and editorial media

- Provide `hero.mp4` and `hero.webm` in `public/assets/video/`, together with a poster image.
- Recommended hero source: 1080p, 8 to 12 seconds, seamless loop, muted, H.264 MP4 plus WebM, each under 6 MB where possible.
- Supply rights-cleared lookbook photography for collections and the About page.

## Policy and legal copy

- Confirm delivery areas, delivery fees, lead times, exchange window, warranty coverage, exclusions, and contact address.
- Review Privacy and Terms pages against the business's actual data-handling and payment processes.
- Provide a consent-approved marketing email policy for newsletter signup.

## SEO and launch

- Confirm production canonical domain and all social-card images.
- Provide Google Search Console and analytics preferences, if required.
- Generate the final sitemap only after the product catalogue and permanent URLs are confirmed.
