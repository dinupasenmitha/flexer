Customer Protection

Support when a frame is not quite right.

We want you to feel complete confidence in every frame you select. If your chosen piece does not fit your facial dimensions comfortably, we offer a dedicated 48-hour exchange service.

1. Exchange Window

48-Hour Fit Guarantee

Notify our WhatsApp team within 48 hours of courier delivery if you wish to swap for another size or style.

14-Day Gift Protection

Unopened and sealed gift sets can be exchanged within 14 days of purchase.

2. Condition Requirements

To qualify for an exchange, items must meet our hygiene and optical safety standards:

  • Eyewear must be completely unworn, with zero micro-scratches on lenses or temple distortions.
  • All original packaging must be returned intact: protective hard case or zip pouch, microfibre optical cloth, warranty card, and brand sleeve.
  • Prescription lenses fitted specifically to your custom optical values by an optometrist cannot be refunded, though the frame itself may be exchanged.

3. Simple 3-Step Exchange Process

  1. Step 1: Message WhatsApp Care

    Send a message to +94 77 558 0968 quoting your order reference (e.g. FLX-20261031-0001) and photos of the frame.

  2. Step 2: Choose Your Replacement

    Our optometry specialists will guide you to a frame that matches your bridge width and temple requirements.

  3. Step 3: Courier Swap

    Our domestic courier partner will hand over your replacement frame while collecting the returned item at your doorstep.
