2. What Is Covered

  • Spontaneous hinge weld breakage or screw thread failure under normal daily wear.
  • Lens polarization film delamination or spontaneous bubbling not caused by chemical cleaners.
  • Core-wire detachment within acetate temples.
  • Embedded nose pad arm weld fractures on titanium / metal frames.

3. What Is Excluded

  • Normal cosmetic surface wear, superficial scratches from improper storage or sandy pockets.
  • Accidental crushing, stepping, vehicle runover, or deliberate bending.
  • Thermal distortion from leaving sunglasses on hot car dashboards under direct Sri Lankan sun.
  • Damage caused by aggressive alcohol, acetone, or solvent-based lens sprays (use only mild soapy water and microfibre).

4. How to Submit a Claim

No paperwork or complicated bureaucracy. Simply WhatsApp your order reference (e.g. FLX-20261031-0001) alongside clear photos of the issue to +94 77 558 0968. Our optical technicians will inspect and arrange prompt repair or full replacement.
