Trust & Security

Your privacy is protected, by architectural design.

FLEXER operates under strict data privacy principles. We collect only what is essential to package and deliver your eyewear safely across Sri Lanka.

Strict Payment Slip Privacy Guarantee

When completing a bank transfer order, the FLEXER website operates an isolated browser-local file interface.

  • Customer bank receipts and transfer slips are NEVER uploaded to or stored on our web servers.
  • Files are NEVER saved in our database, server logs, or cloud buckets.
  • Files are shared directly by you through your personal device share or attached within WhatsApp.
  • Once shared in WhatsApp, your payment is verified by our finance desk and your order reference is updated to confirmed.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the details needed to process and deliver your order:

  • Full customer name for package labelling.
  • Delivery address, city, and Sri Lankan district for domestic courier dispatch.
  • Telephone and WhatsApp contact number for courier coordination and delivery updates.
  • Email address (optional) if you subscribe to drop announcements.

2. How Your Data Is Handled

Your delivery information is shared strictly with registered Sri Lankan domestic couriers solely for fulfilling delivery. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize customer data with third-party advertising networks.

3. Data Rights & Retention

In alignment with Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you may request a copy of your stored order records or ask for your contact information to be removed after order fulfilment is complete by messaging care@flexer.lk or our WhatsApp Care line (+94 77 558 0968).
