The Origin & Philosophy

Frames for the way you move through the world.

FLEXER is a Sri Lankan eyewear destination born from a commitment to optometry-backed clarity, contemporary aesthetics, and personal customer care.

Optometry-Informed From Day One

Flex was born from a simple observation in Sri Lanka: while ordinary sunglasses and frames were easy to locate across street vendors and retail malls, a truly considered, high-aesthetic eyewear experience was nearly impossible to find.

Founded by an optometry and marketing scholar, FLEXER was started with a dedicated mission: to combine ophthalmic optical principles with refined frame aesthetics and unboxing presentation at fair Sri Lankan prices.

“Quality is our qualification, not our marketing slogan.”

Optical Standards & UV400 Clarity

Because of our foundation in ophthalmic optics, we understand the critical necessity of genuine UV400 radiation shielding, optical center alignment, and frame ergonomic balance. We strictly reject poorly molded plastics and inferior acrylic lenses that distort vision and fatigue the eyes.

  • Sunglasses: Every pair blocks 99.9% of harmful UVA and UVB radiation (UV400 Category 3), fitted with scratch-resistant, impact-absorbing TAC polarized lenses.
  • Optical Frames: Engineered with precision alloy rimlock screws and ergonomic acetate bridges to receive custom prescription lenses from your licensed optometrist.
  • Blue-Light Filtering: Computer and screen demo lenses minimize eye strain during long digital work hours.
