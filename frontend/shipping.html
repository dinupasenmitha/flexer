3. Dispatch Inspection & Packaging

Prior to boxing, each frame undergoes a hands-on optometry standard check:

  • Hinge screw tension and temple balance are aligned.
  • Lenses are inspected under high-intensity optical light for zero surface abrasions.
  • Enclosed in our hard protective case or zip pouch, microfibre care cloth, and sealed package.
